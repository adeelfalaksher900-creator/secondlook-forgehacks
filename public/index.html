KEEP YOUR NEXT STEP SIMPLE

Verify through a
channel you trust.

A convincing message is not proof of identity.

01

Pause the conversation

Don't send money, passwords, or one-time codes while you check. Don't follow a link just because it appears in a message.

02

Find your own contact

Use an official app, a saved contact, or a website address you already know. Avoid contact details supplied in the suspicious message.

03

Ask about the request

Confirm both the identity and the specific action being requested. A familiar name, logo, voice, or writing style can be impersonated.

04

If you've already acted

Contact the service through its official support channel. If money was sent, contact your payment provider promptly. Change exposed passwords through the official app.

Know the limits

Trained on the historical UCI SMS Spam Collection. Spam labels are not fraud labels. These results do not establish performance on modern scams, emails, or AI impersonation.

Duplicates were removed before the split. Near-duplicates may remain. Short, unfamiliar, and non-English messages receive a limited-context notice.

No external URL reputation checks, identity verification, or AI-content detection. No claimed reduction in fraud.
