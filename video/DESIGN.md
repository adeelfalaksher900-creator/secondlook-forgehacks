# SecondLook design direction
A quiet editorial workspace that helps a reader pause. Light warm paper, deep forest ink, restrained amber evidence, clear typography. Interface screenshots are actual product states, not illustrative mockups. No generic neon cybersecurity imagery or stock people.
## Colors
- #f5f6f2 paper
- #172b2a primary ink
- #245b47 actions and accents
- #536763 secondary text
- #87612a evidence emphasis
## Typography
Georgia for display; Arial for controls and body. Generous whitespace, subtle borders, twelve-pixel card radii.
## Motion
Short result entrance, disabled under reduced motion. Video: calm, continuous real app interaction. Minimal titles and transitions.
## What NOT to Do
No safe verdicts, blinking warnings, fake metrics, decorative hacker imagery, fabricated screenshots, or invented user endorsements.
